A FEW
CLARIFICATIONS.

Where can I play Civic Watch?

Civic Watch is coming to iPhone and iPad. Public beta access is currently closed. An App Store release date has not been announced here.

Can I play without an internet connection?

Yes. Bot deathmatch and the gun range work offline. Online free-for-all, purchases and Apple services require an internet connection.

Do characters have different abilities?

No. All selectable operatives share the same combat stats and living hitbox dimensions. The different silhouettes and biographies are cosmetic. Some operatives are optional purchases.

What about controls and progression?

Play with landscape touch and optional gyro on iPhone and iPad, keyboard and mouse on Mac or PC, or an Xbox controller on mobile, Mac, and PC. See the labeled controller layout. Fine-tune sensitivity and aim assist in Settings. Earn career XP, climb ranks 1–100 and review accuracy, headshots and K/D after a match.

Is this a story campaign?

Fairview’s backstory sets the stage for arena deathmatch. The game offers offline bot matches, online free-for-all and a practice range; this site is not announcing a story campaign.

How do I get help or restore purchases?

Open Settings → Profile → Restore Purchases in the game. For help, visit player support or email joseph@codecreations.net.
