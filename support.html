How to play

  1. From the main menu pick an arena, bot count, difficulty, game mode, and frag limit. Normal is tankier (bullets at 42% power), Advanced is standard, Hardcore is one-shot.
  2. Start a bot deathmatch anytime — no account required. Six operatives are free: Rook, Crane, Floyd, Eric, Brenda, and Preston.
  3. For online free-for-all, host a lobby and share the party code, or join with a code.
  4. On the same Wi‑Fi, local-network discovery can list nearby hosts.
  5. Open the gun range from the home screen to try weapons, optics, and the laser module.

Controls

Civic Watch is ready for mobile, keyboard and mouse, and gaming controllers. Play with landscape touch and optional gyro on iPhone and iPad. Use keyboard and mouse on Mac or PC. Xbox controllers work on iPhone, iPad, Mac, and PC. Tap a diagram to enlarge.

FAQ

Is this the official support page?

Yes. Contact joseph@codecreations.net for help with Civic Watch, purchases, or online matches.

Can I play alone?

Yes. Bot deathmatch works offline with up to ten AI opponents. The gun range is also offline.

Are there in-app purchases?

Optional. Extra operatives are $3.99. Solid gun finishes are $1.99 and animated finishes are $2.99. Watch Coin packs ($0.99–$19.99) and Zombies supplies (self-revive, continue, loadout, perk, airstrike) are consumable and only affect the Zombies mode. The factory finish and six operatives stay free. There are no loot boxes and no subscriptions.

How do I restore purchases?

Settings → Profile → Restore Purchases, signed into the Apple Account that bought them. That re-grants non-consumable operatives and finishes. Watch Coins and Zombies supplies are consumable and cannot be restored. We never delete a purchase you own.

How do I report or block a player?

In an online match, open the scoreboard and tap the other player’s name, then Report or Block. Settings → Profile also lists blocked players. You can email joseph@codecreations.net. There is no voice chat.

Is there voice chat?

No. Civic Watch does not use the microphone.

The game only works in landscape

Correct. Civic Watch is sensor-landscape only (both landscape directions). Portrait is not supported.

Game Center

Optional. Settings → Profile opens Achievements and Leaderboards on iOS. Sign-in is not required to play bot matches.

Something crashed

Email joseph@codecreations.net with the time it happened. If you can, Settings → Privacy & Security → Analytics & Improvements → Analytics Data, and attach the latest crash.

Delete my data

See Privacy. No separate Civic Watch account is required. Email us to request access to or deletion of information we hold.

Is this related to GoldenEye / Bond / Nintendo?

No. Civic Watch is an original game by Solution Sidekick LLC and is not affiliated with Nintendo, Rare, or any Bond franchise.
